**WACC mechanical explanation only:** CAPM cost of equity = assumed risk-free 4.0% + 1.50 beta * assumed 6.5% ERP = 13.75%. Assumed pretax borrowing rate 6.5% × (1-18% illustrative tax) = 5.33%. Illustrative capital weights 72% equity / 28% debt yield 11.39%, rounded 11.4%. **None of these component inputs are verified October 19 estimates** and the components were selected only to illustrate a plausible identity for the carried-forward 11.4%. This cannot be described as sourced market WACC.

**Normalization boundary:** Oracle's reported FY2026 net income includes approx. $2.7bn non-operating Ampere disposal gain. A simple after-tax exclusion using *assumed* 18% gives $14.873bn normalized proxy, not GAAP. The forward pro forma primarily uses operating EBIT; one-time *non-operating* gain must not be layered into recurring EBIT or automatically into cash FCFF. Other reclassifications/one-offs must be checked before calling the base fully normalized.
